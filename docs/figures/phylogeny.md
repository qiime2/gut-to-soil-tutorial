**A rooted phylogeny of the ASV sequences.** A rooted phylogenetic tree relating the eleven ASV sequences. Horizontal distances are drawn to scale and every branch is labelled with its length in substitutions per site; vertical placement carries no information. Relatedness-based diversity metrics can use a phylogenetic tree to represent the relatedness between features.
