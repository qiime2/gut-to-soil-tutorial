**Alpha rarefaction plot of observed features as a function of even sampling depth.** For an alpha rarefaction plot (panel **a**), the feature table is subsampled without replacement at evenly spaced depths and a specific alpha diversity metric is computed on each of those tables. In this plot, there are 20 even sampling depths ranging from 1 to 10,000, and the observed features metric is calculated on each feature table. This allows you to assess several aspects of the data, including relative alpha diversity of the samples, the even sampling depth at which each sample appears to have been "fully sampled" (meaning that additional sequences do not yield additional alpha diversity), and the sampling depth at which specific samples are dropped. The dashed vertical lines mark 1800 and 8000, the two candidate even sampling depths explored in [](#fig-even-sampling), each labelled with the number of samples that would be retained if that sampling depth was selected. QIIME 2's `alpha-rarefaction` command repeats each even sampling depth 10 times and plots the median value for each (sample, even sampling depth) pair. Panels **b** and **c** mirror those generated by QIIME 2's `alpha-rarefaction` command, where samples are generally grouped into single lines by metadata. In panel **b**, our HEC samples are all grouped together, and in this view it is not immediately clear how many samples are retained at each even sampling depth. Panel **c** illustrates this information.
